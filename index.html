    02 About

    I work as a data analyst in the public sector, where I spend my days in Python and SQL, turning raw data into dashboards that make complex information easy to understand and act on. I value precision and structure, whether that means a clean dataset or a well organized spreadsheet.

    That same mindset carries over into my time outside of work. I collect hobbies as much as things: building custom keyboards, experimenting with 3D printing, shooting photography, and thinking through interior design. I enjoy gaming, collect cards and try to stay active whenever I can.

    Across all of it, a few things stay constant: I like things organized, I enjoy a bit of healthy competition, and I'm always looking for creative ways to bring an idea to life. This site is where I bring it all together.
